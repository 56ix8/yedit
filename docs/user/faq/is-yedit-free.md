---
title: Is YEDIT free?
description: YEDIT is free and open source under the MIT or Apache 2.0 license, and works offline.
order: 10
verified: 0.15.1
---

Yes. The code is licensed under MIT or Apache 2.0, your choice, and is on [GitHub](https://github.com/56ix8/yedit). YEDIT is based on [PhotoCraft](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft), which uses the same licenses.

YEDIT works offline. Its smart selection tools run on your computer, with no cloud and no account.

See [Install YEDIT](../getting-started/install.md) to download it.
